The Problem with Classic Survivor

One Week at a Time
Gets Old Fast.

Classic survivor is great — but it has a fundamental flaw. You don't really engage with the season. You just tune in weekly, make a pick, and wait.

No Season-Long Strategy

In classic survivor, you're making decisions one week at a time with no view of the whole season. The strategy is shallow — pick the biggest favorite and hope for the best.

Dead Weeks

Once you're eliminated — which for most players happens early — the season is over for you. There's no reason to stay engaged with the pool you're no longer in.

Slow Build, Slow Payoff

You wait all summer for football to start, then spend the first few months making cautious picks. The real excitement doesn't arrive until November — if you even make it that far.

No Preseason Engagement

The NFL offseason is full of storylines — trades, drafts, injuries, schedules. Classic survivor doesn't give you any reason to care about any of it. You just wait for Week 1.

How It Works

Lock In Before
the Season Starts.

Pick Your Path flips the script. Instead of picking week by week, you map out your entire season strategy in August — then watch it play out in real time.

01

Build Your Path

Before Week 1, you assign a team to every week of the season. Week 3 you'll use the Eagles. Week 9 you'll save for the Chiefs. You see the whole board.

02

Watch It Unfold

Once the season starts, your path is locked in. Each week, your pre-assigned pick plays out — wins keep you alive, losses eliminate you, just like classic survivor.

03

Outsmart the Field

The winner isn't just the luckiest — it's the player who mapped the cleverest path in August. Preseason research actually pays off.

Why It Changes Everything

Action All
Season Long.

Pick Your Path turns a weekly ritual into a season-long story. Every game, every result, every trade moves your path in real time.

Start in August

The moment the schedule drops, Pick Your Path begins. Debate matchups, study bye weeks, and obsess over early-season home games — all before a single snap is played.

Every Game Has Stakes

Because your picks are locked in, you have a team to root for every single week of the NFL season — even on a random Thursday in November.

Strategy That Rewards Research

Who has the softest schedule in Weeks 10–14? Which division plays each other twice in a row? Pick Your Path rewards the fans who actually dig into the data.

The Drama of the Long Game

You'll watch a team you picked for Week 11 trade away their best receiver in October and feel it in your gut. Your path is personal. The season becomes your story.
