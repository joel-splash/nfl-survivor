The Format

What is NFL Survivor?

Survivor contests are one of the oldest and most beloved formats in football. Here's how it works.

01

Pick One Team

Each week of the NFL season, you pick one team you think will win their game. Just one.

02

Each Team Once

The catch: you can only use each team once all season. Pick the Chiefs in Week 1? They're gone. Forever.

03

Survive or Go Home

Lose one week — even by a single point — and you're out. Last person standing takes the prize.

Why Everyone Gets Hooked

Simple to Start.
Impossible to Stop.

Survivor combines the tension of a bracket, the strategy of season-long fantasy, and the stakes of a weekly bet — all in one pick.

Every Game Matters

You don't need your team to cover a spread or your RB to hit 100 yards. You just need your team to win — and suddenly a Thursday night game between two middling teams becomes unmissable.

The Team Bank Problem

By midseason, your "safe" picks are all spent. The Chiefs, Eagles, and Cowboys are gone. Now you're sweating a Cincinnati-at-Baltimore game on your last good pick. That tension is the whole game.

Last Longer with Your Crew

Survivor is best played with a group. There's nothing like watching a buddy get eliminated in Week 2 on a sure thing — or being the last one standing in January with everyone's money in your pocket.

Massive Upside

Entry fees are small. Prizes get big when a full office joins the contest. And because most players flame out early, the final few weeks are winner-take-all chaos with real money on the line.
