The Format

What is NFL Survivor?

Survivor pools are one of the oldest and most beloved formats in football. Here's how it works.

01

Pick One Team

Each week of the NFL season, you pick one team you think will win their game. Just one.

02

Each Team Once

The catch: you can only use each team once all season. Pick the Chiefs in Week 1? They're gone. Forever.

03

Survive or Go Home

Lose one week — even by a single point — and you're out. Last person standing takes the pot.

Why Everyone Gets Hooked

Simple to Start.
Impossible to Stop.

Survivor combines the tension of a bracket, the strategy of season-long fantasy, and the stakes of a weekly bet — all in one pick.

Every Game Matters

You don't need your team to cover a spread or your RB to hit 100 yards. You just need your team to win — and suddenly a Thursday night game between two middling teams becomes unmissable.

The Team Bank Problem

By midseason, your "safe" picks are all spent. The Chiefs, Eagles, and Cowboys are gone. Now you're sweating a Cincinnati-at-Baltimore game on your last good pick. That tension is the whole game.

Last Longer with Your Crew

Survivor is best played with a group. There's nothing like watching a buddy get eliminated in Week 2 on a sure thing — or being the last one standing in January with everyone's money in your pocket.

Massive Upside

Entry fees are small. Prize pools get big when a full office pool participates. And because most players flame out early, the final few weeks are winner-take-all chaos with real money on the line.

For the Serious Player

Survivor Strategy

Think you can just pick the best team every week? The players who go deep know better. Here's what separates the survivors from the eliminated.

Save Your Best Teams

The Chiefs will always be available early. Save them for a Week 14 trap game when everyone else is scrambling. Blowing Kansas City in Week 1 on a game they win by 30 is the most common survivor mistake.

Shop the Schedule

Before the season, map out when your favorite teams face the weakest opponents. A great matchup in Week 10 is worth protecting now. Your goal isn't to pick the best team each week — it's to always have a good pick available.

Beware Trap Games

A 9-1 team playing on the road against a 4-6 divisional rival in a cold-weather stadium after a bye is a trap. The lines look fine. People get eliminated. Respect the context, not just the record.

Watch the Field

In group pools, if 70% of the field picks the same team and they lose, you just gained ground on everyone. Sometimes the contrarian pick — even a slightly riskier one — is worth it for field position.
